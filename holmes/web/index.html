✦ The Irregulars a Sherlock Holmes case: one detective against the Baker Street Irregulars

Who solves a murder better: one genius detective, or the Baker Street Irregulars?

Each case is a murder in Victorian London with a hidden culprit, motive and hideout. Every address on the map holds one short lead: a witness, a receipt, a footprint, a lie, or nothing useful. Choose the investigator(s) (step 1), set up the mystery case (step 2), then press Start the investigation and watch it live.
