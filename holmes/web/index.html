The Irregulars jev-swarm · a seeded Holmes case, solved by one detective or a swarm

A body has been found somewhere in London. Each address on the map holds one short lead: a witness, a receipt, a footprint, a lie or nothing at all. Solo: one detective visits one address at a time and then names the culprit, motive and hideout. Swarm: Wiggins's Irregulars spread over the city, each seeing only its own corner and what neighbours shout, and the case closes when a quorum votes the same answer. Pick a preset, adjust the knobs, then launch.
