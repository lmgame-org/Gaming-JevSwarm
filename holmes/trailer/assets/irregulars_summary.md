Wanting to share some fun hack by putting a side by side comparison among Astra, Astra leader + Jev swarm, Jev swarm in Holmes detective game.

Setup: a Victorian London murder game (100 addresses, 10 suspects, some witnesses lie). Solve = culprit, motive and hideout right within 12 case hours.
- The Lone Genius (GPT6-Astra): one detective, 20 visits.
- Holmes (GPT6-Astra) + Jev Swarm: Astra directs 12 Jev boys who run errands.
- Jev Swarm: 100 Jev boys, no leader; they share clues and vote, closing at 30 agreeing.

Interesting findings (100 cases each):
1. Leader + swarm wins: 96/100 solved, vs 57 for the Lone Genius and 38 for the Jev Swarm.
2. The Lone Genius lacks legwork, not brains: with 2x time and visits it reaches 91, but needs ~15 case hours and ~2 min per case.
3. The Jev Swarm agrees fast (median 2.2 case hours) but often wrongly: 53 of its 91 agreements were wrong.
4. When somebody lies (frame-up cases): Holmes (GPT6-Astra) + Jev Swarm 93, Lone Genius 59, Jev Swarm 42, and a clue-counting Holmes + heuristic Swarm 1. Counting follows the majority; only a reasoning leader sees through the lie.
5. On honest cases, a counting Holmes + Jev Swarm does as well (95) and ~20x faster.
