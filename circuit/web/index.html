⌁ Circuit debugging a pipelined CPU: one debugger, Holmes and his street boys, or a leaderless swarm

Who finds a hardware bug: one debugger, Holmes with his boys, or a leaderless swarm?

The CPU is an 8-bit, 5-stage pipeline of 96 blocks. Each bug number hides one bug (sometimes two) in one of the 49 logic blocks. The investigators only learn which registers end up wrong. Each probe costs 15 minutes of bench time, about one reading in twenty is glitched, and some boys and Irregulars are bribed. Someone must name the broken block, the signal, the bug type and the fix; the fix is graded by re-running every test.
