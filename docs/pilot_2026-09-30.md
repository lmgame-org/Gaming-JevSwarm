# Pilot, 30 Sep 2026

These pilot runs use the doc's starting parameters, which are not yet tuned or frozen. Setup: scenario `demo`
(flank attack at 20 s, withdrawal at 80 s), 64 fish, controlled mode, seeds 1 to 5, and the same seeds for every
policy. Values are episode means ± 95% t-interval (n = 5).

| metric | jev | local_rule | local_react | random | fixed_school |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| survival fraction | 0.653 ± 0.086 | **0.750 ± 0.057** | 0.650 ± 0.033 | 0.534 ± 0.035 | 0.466 ± 0.109 |
| captures | 22.2 ± 5.5 | **16.0 ± 3.6** | 22.4 ± 2.1 | 29.8 ± 2.2 | 34.2 ± 7.0 |
| time to first capture (s) | 27.4 ± 2.4 | 32.1 ± 2.0 | 28.6 ± 2.3 | 26.5 ± 2.4 | 27.0 ± 1.8 |
| largest group fraction (end) | 0.49 ± 0.24 | 0.79 ± 0.28 | 0.69 ± 0.25 | 0.90 ± 0.07 | 1.00 |
| polarization (end) | 0.26 ± 0.21 | 0.83 ± 0.25 | 0.24 ± 0.13 | 0.13 ± 0.08 | 0.99 ± 0.03 |
| recovered after withdrawal | 0 / 5 | 2 / 5 | 0 / 5 | 0 / 5 | 5 / 5 (never split) |
| mean energy (end) | 0.79 | 0.61 | 0.33 | 0.19 | 1.00 |
| contacts per fish-minute | 5.0 | 1.3 | 19.1 | 7.4 | 0.07 |

**Jev inference.** Every decision came from Jev (0% fallback). Latency p50 was about 0.24 s, and all five episodes
cost $2.75 in total. The action mix was CONTINUE 28%, SCAN 20%, ALIGN 18%, FOLLOW 16%, COHERE 9%, RECOVER 6%,
ESCAPE 2% and BURST 0.7%.

**Reading.** At these settings Jev beats the random and fixed-school selectors, ties the neighbour-reacting rule, and
does worse than the simple school-and-flee rule (0.65 vs 0.75 survival). The visible failure mode is loss of cohesion.
Jev fish split into several groups (largest group 0.49) and never regrouped after the predator left. It rarely chooses
ESCAPE, and it often chooses CONTINUE or SCAN while other fish are nearby.

## Causal check: branch at the first local escape (Jev, seeds 1 to 5, 3 s horizon)

This compares two branches from the first local escape: one keeps the witness's escape (permit) and one replaces it
with CONTINUE for one interval (suppress). The table shows permit minus suppress.

| outcome among fish that never saw a predator | permit − suppress |
|---|---|
| fish that applied BURST | +0.8 ± 3.3 |
| fish that reached fast-swim speed | +0.0 ± 2.9 |
| evaluator indirect responses | +6.6 ± 13.8 |

No propagation effect is detectable at n = 5. After the branch point Jev is queried live, so the two branches also
differ through Jev's own sampling variability, which widens the intervals.

## Escape cues: do Jev fish join a visible neighbour's escape? (added 30 Sep, evening)

**Before this change they did not.** In the pilot, when a fish saw a fast neighbour (≥ 3 BL/s) and no predator, Jev
chose to follow that neighbour or burst in only 4% of decisions, about the same as when no neighbour was fast. There
were two causes. FOLLOW was offered only for the 3 closest fish, and the fast fish was one of them in only 41% of cases.
Jev also saw only raw speed numbers, with no reference for what counts as fast.

**What changed.** A new `escape_cues` setting is on by default and can be switched off on the launch screen. It uses
only the fish's own estimates of what it sees:

- **Motion label:** each visible neighbour gets a `motion` label: `fast`, `sharp_turn`, `normal` or `unknown`. The
  thresholds match the evaluator's cue definitions: speed above the fish's own cruise speed + 1 BL/s, or a turn of at
  least 60°/s.
- **Own speeds:** the fish's own cruise and burst speeds are added to `self`.
- **FOLLOW options:** FOLLOW is also offered for any fast or sharply turning neighbour, up to 5 FOLLOW options in total.
- **New action:** `FLEE_WITH nX` bursts along the observed heading of a fast or sharply turning neighbour.
- **Instruction:** the decision instruction gains one sentence saying such a neighbour may be escaping an unseen
  predator, and that it may also be a false alarm.

**Result on the same 5 seeds.** Values are episode means ± 95% t-interval.

| | Jev before | Jev with cues | local_react with cues |
|---|---|---|---|
| joins a fast neighbour (FOLLOW it, FLEE_WITH or BURST), no predator visible | 4% | **40%** | rule |
| survival fraction | 0.653 ± 0.086 | 0.694 ± 0.142 | 0.688 ± 0.033 |
| captures | 22.2 | 19.6 | 20.0 |
| largest group fraction (end) | 0.49 | 0.63 | 0.47 |
| mean energy (end) | 0.79 | **0.20** | 0.21 |
| bursts before any predator exists (first 20 s) | 0.7% | **30.5%** | |

Jev fish now join visible escapes, and survival moves up slightly, though the change is within noise at n = 5. The cost
is **false-alarm amplification**. Before any predator appears, one fish's burst makes its neighbours flee, and their
bursts trigger further fleeing, so energy ends at 0.20 instead of 0.79. This is the trade-off the doc's false-alarm
scenario is designed to measure. The five episodes with cues cost $3.59.
